Free Roof Inspection

What you get.

What free means here

No fee, and you don't owe us a thing after. No 'special price if you sign tonight.' If the roof's fine, you don't get a sales pitch.

Why 21 points

Kicking a shingle tells you nothing. We check 21 specific things and photograph the problems, so you see what we see.

We go in the attic too

Soft decking, bad airflow, moisture and daylight show up from the inside first. So do ceiling stains. Somebody needs to be home to let us in.

Proof, not pressure

Some outfits use a free inspection to scare people into a roof. You get photos from your own roof, and you decide on your own time.

Buying or selling?

A buyer's inspector flagged the roof? We'll look and give you a straight read, with photos you can share.

The 21 points

What we check, point by point.

12 up top, 3 around the edges and 6 underneath and inside.

Up top

  1. Shingle condition (cracking, curling, missing)
  2. Granule loss
  3. Nail pops and exposed fasteners
  4. Ridge cap and ridge vent
  5. Valleys
  6. Pipe boots and vent collars
  7. Chimney flashing
  8. Step flashing and wall flashing
  9. Skylight seals and flashing
  10. Drip edge
  11. Moss, algae, and debris
  12. Old repairs and patches

Around the edges

  1. Gutters and downspouts
  2. Fascia and soffit
  3. Siding where it meets the roofline

Underneath and inside

  1. Decking soft spots
  2. Attic ventilation
  3. Attic moisture, mold, or daylight showing through
  4. Insulation condition
  5. Ceiling and wall stains inside the house
  6. Storm damage (hail, wind, fallen limbs)

Free Roof Inspection FAQ

Free 21-point roof inspection questions.

Straight answers, no runaround.

Get a quote

Is the roof inspection really free?

Yes. No fee, and you don't owe us a thing after. You get photos and a straight answer. If the roof needs work, you get a written quote. If it doesn't, we tell you that and leave.

What does a roof inspection consist of?

Ours is 21 points: 12 up top (shingles, granules, nail pops, ridge, valleys, pipe boots, flashing, skylights, drip edge, moss and old patches), 3 around the edges (gutters, fascia and soffit, siding at the roofline) and 6 underneath and inside (decking, attic airflow, attic moisture, insulation, ceiling stains and storm damage). The full list is on this page.

Is a free roof inspection a scam?

It can be, when somebody uses it to sell a roof you don't need. That's why ours comes with photos of every problem we find, from your own roof. You see it with your own eyes, and the decision is yours.

Do I need to be home?

Yes, for the inside part. We check the attic and the ceilings, so somebody has to let us in.

How often should a roof get inspected?

After any big wind storm, before you buy or sell a house, and every so often once the roof gets older. If you don't know how old yours is, that's a good reason to check.

Can I use the photos for an insurance claim?

The photos are yours to share with anyone, your insurance company included. Your insurance company decides what's covered.
