Roofs

Roofs first.

Roof Replacement

Old roof off, down to the wood. Bad decking out. New roof on, with new flashing at the walls and pipes, and your yard cleaned up like we were never there. It starts with a free 21-point inspection, so you see the photos before you spend a dime.

Roof Replacement

Roof Repair

Most roofs don't need replacing. They need somebody who'll find the leak. We trace it to the source, fix it and show you the photo. If it's a repair, we sell you a repair.

Roof Repair

Free Roof Inspection

We get on your roof, check 21 things up top, around the edges and in the attic, and hand you photos of what we found. Needs a repair? We say so. Needs a new roof? We say that too. Roof's fine? You get on with your day.

Free Roof Inspection

Storm Damage

Wind took shingles. A limb came down. Water's getting in. Tarp first, then dated photos and a written repair estimate you can give your insurance company. Then we fix it right.

Storm Damage

Emergency Tarping

Tree on the roof? Wind ripped a section open and it's raining in? Call (732) 344-4702. A tarp keeps the water out until the real repair, and we come back to inspect the roof and quote the real fix.

Emergency Tarping

Flat Roofs

Porch roofs, additions, the low-slope section over the back room. Flat roofs hold water, and water finds seams. We repair them, replace them, and tell you which one makes sense.

Flat Roofs
Exteriors

The rest of the outside.

Siding

Siding does 2 jobs: keeps water out of your walls and makes the house look like somebody cares. We fix damaged sections, re-side whole houses, and replace the rotted trim and soffit hiding behind it (add-ons in the table).

Siding

Gutters

Gutters that overflow or pull away from the house dump water at your foundation and rot your fascia. We put up new seamless gutters, re-hang the sagging ones, and add guards so you're up the ladder a lot less.

Gutters

Windows

Drafty, foggy or painted shut? We put replacement windows in your existing openings and can cap the outside trim for an added price. Vinyl, premium vinyl or fiberglass, with ballpark ranges up front and the real price in writing.

Windows

Decks & Porches

New decks, deck repairs and porches, in wood or composite. You get a written quote, and when the job needs a permit, it gets pulled before we start.

Decks & Porches
