Roofing in Tinton Falls

Tinton Falls, the way we see it.

Townhomes and HOAs

Only 44% of Tinton Falls' 9,124 homes are detached. 25% are attached townhomes and 24% are in buildings of 5 or more units. In a townhome row, roofs and siding are often the association's call, so start with your HOA's rules.

Siding on 80s and 90s houses

Houses from the late 1980s and the 1990s are reaching the age where original vinyl siding can get brittle, faded or loose. That's where our siding work comes in.

The historic district

Tinton Falls has a local historic district around Sycamore Avenue, Tinton Avenue, Water Street and Old Mill Road, with a Historic Preservation Commission. The borough code calls for its review on exterior work in public view there, so check with the borough first if your house is in that area.

Permits and storms

The Tinton Falls Borough Building Department is at 556 Tinton Avenue (732-542-3400, ext. 238). Weather records cited in Monmouth County's 2020 hazard mitigation plan list an F2 tornado in Tinton Falls on Oct. 16, 1955.
