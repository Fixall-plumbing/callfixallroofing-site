Decks & Porches FAQ

Decks & Porches questions.

Straight answers, no runaround.

Get a quote

Do I need a permit to build a deck in NJ?

A new deck, yes. Repairing or replacing parts of a deck, porch or stoop that don't hold up a roof is ordinary maintenance (N.J.A.C. 5:23-2.7). When your job needs a permit, we pull it before work starts and you get the inspection certificates before final payment.

Wood or composite decking?

Wood costs less up front and needs sealing or stain over the years. Composite costs more and doesn't need stain, though it still needs cleaning. Ask us to price both and compare.

How much does a deck cost in NJ?

It depends on size, height, material, stairs and rails. We don't post deck ranges yet. You get a written quote after we measure.

Can you repair my deck instead of replacing it?

If the framing and posts are sound, often yes: new boards, rails or stairs. If the posts or joists are rotted, replacement is the safer call.

Do you build porches?

Yes, porches and porch repairs, and porch roofs too.
